What Drives Me

As a developer, I face all kinds of challenges: bugs, unexplained broken functionality, and messy and undocumented code.
I won't lie, there have been moments when I wanted to throw my laptop out of the window.

But here's the thing. I always come back.

Because underneath the frustration and endless debugging lies something that motivates me every day: the ability to create.
I became a developer because I was amazed that a few lines of code could bring ideas to life. That you could wake up with a concept and have a working prototype by night. That magic "turning thoughts into tools, and ideas into interfaces" is what got me, and keeps me interested!

"That One Line of Code That Took Three Hours"

Even when I spend three hours chasing down a single broken line of code, I keep going.
There’s nothing like that moment when everything finally clicks, the app runs flawlessly, and I whisper to myself, "I am a genius” right before realizing... it was a missing function call all along. 😅

I don’t code just to fix bugs.
I do it, because I love chasing that “aha” moment.

I thrive on learning, experimenting, and constantly figuring out how to make systems better. Not just for myself, but for the people who use them. It’s rarely easy. But it’s always worth it.
