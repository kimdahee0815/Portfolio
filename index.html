What Drives Me

💡 Curiosity is what starts every journey for me. A simple question often grows into a project, and every project teaches me something new.

🐞 I believe the best learning happens during debugging. Every bug gives me a reason to ask "why?", uncover how things really work, and discover something I didn't know before.
